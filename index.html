About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at McGill University, advised by Prof. Brett H. Meyer and Prof. Warren J. Gross. My research lies broadly in deep learning, efficient AI systems, and machine learning optimization, with interests in TinyML, model compression, and neural architecture search.

My goal is to build practical AI models that are not only accurate, but also efficient, deployable, and scalable for real-world systems.

Outside of research, I enjoy snowboarding, scuba diving, and playing tennis.

Research

My current research interests include efficient and practical machine learning systems for real-world deployment.

  • Efficient AI / TinyML
